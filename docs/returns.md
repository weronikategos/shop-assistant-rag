# Returns and complaints

## Returning a product
You can return any product within **14 days** of receiving it, without giving a reason. Accessories (drippers, grinders, mugs) must be unused and in the original packaging. Opened coffee and tea can't be returned for hygiene reasons, unless they are faulty.

To start a return, write to returns@coffeeshop.example with your order number. We'll reply with the return address. You pay for sending the return yourself, unless the product was faulty or we sent the wrong item.

## Refunds
We refund the money within **14 days** of receiving the returned product, to the same payment method you used. The original delivery cost is refunded too, up to the price of the cheapest delivery option.

## Complaints (faulty products)
Accessories have a **2-year** warranty. If a product stops working or arrives faulty, write to us with your order number, a short description and a photo. We answer every complaint within 14 days and either repair, replace or refund the product.

The hand coffee grinder has a **3-year** warranty on its steel burrs.

## Coffee that tastes wrong
If a coffee tastes stale or off, tell us within 30 days of delivery. We'll send a new bag from a fresh roast at no cost, without asking you to send the old one back.
