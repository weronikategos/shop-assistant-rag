# Sweets

## Dark chocolate 70% 100 g (18.99 PLN)
Single-origin cocoa from Peru. It pairs well with espresso. Ingredients: cocoa mass, sugar, cocoa butter. It may contain traces of nuts and milk.

## Almond cantuccini 200 g (22.50 PLN)
Crunchy Italian biscuits for dipping in coffee. Ingredients include wheat flour, almonds (18%), sugar and eggs. Not suitable for people allergic to nuts or gluten.

## Marzipan pralines 150 g (34.00 PLN)
Handmade marzipan pralines coated in dark chocolate. They contain almonds. Best before date: 3 months from production; store at 15–20 °C.

We don't offer gluten-free or vegan sweets at the moment.
