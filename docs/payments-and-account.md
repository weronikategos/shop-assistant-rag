# Payments and your account

## Payment methods
We accept BLIK, card payments (Visa, Mastercard), fast bank transfers and Google Pay. All payments go through a payment operator, so we never see or store your card details.

Cash on delivery is not available.

## Invoices
Every order comes with an electronic receipt. If you need a VAT invoice, tick "I need an invoice" at checkout and enter your company's tax number (NIP). Invoices can't be issued after the order has been placed.

## Discount codes
One discount code can be used per order. Codes don't apply to the delivery cost and can't be combined with the free shipping threshold if they bring the order value below 150 PLN.

## Your account
You need an account to place an order. Passwords must have at least 8 characters, and we store them only in hashed form. In "My orders" you can see the history and status of every order.

If you forget your password, use "Forgot password" on the login page. To delete your account and data, write to hello@coffeeshop.example. We remove the data within 30 days, except what we must keep for accounting.
