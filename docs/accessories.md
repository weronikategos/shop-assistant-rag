# Accessories

## V60 dripper (89.00 PLN)
A ceramic pour-over dripper, size 02, for 1–4 cups. It fits standard size 02 paper filters, which are not included. Ceramic keeps heat well, so rinse it with hot water before brewing.

**Basic V60 recipe:** 15 g of coffee ground medium-fine (like sea salt), 250 g of water at 92–96 °C. Pour about 40 g of water, wait 30 seconds (the "bloom"), then pour the rest slowly in circles. Total brewing time should be around 2:30–3:00. If it's sour, grind finer; if it's bitter, grind coarser.

## Hand coffee grinder (149.00 PLN)
A manual grinder with steel conical burrs and stepless grind adjustment, holding up to 30 g of beans. Turn the adjustment ring clockwise for a finer grind. It can grind fine enough for espresso and coarse enough for a French press.

**Cleaning:** brush the burrs after use and never wash them with water, because they can rust. Once a month, take the burrs out and clean them with a dry brush. The burrs have a 3-year warranty.

## Travel mug 350 ml (59.00 PLN)
A stainless steel, vacuum-insulated mug. It keeps drinks hot for about 6 hours and cold for about 12 hours. The lid is leak-proof when closed. Wash the mug by hand; it is **not dishwasher safe**, and don't use it in a microwave.
