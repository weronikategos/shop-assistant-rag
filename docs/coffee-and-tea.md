# Our coffee and tea

## Brazil whole bean coffee 250 g (39.99 PLN)
A chocolatey coffee with low acidity, roasted medium. Good for espresso, moka pots and milk drinks. Origin: Minas Gerais, Brazil, natural process.

## Ethiopia whole bean coffee 250 g (49.99 PLN)
A fruity, light roast with notes of berries and jasmine. Best brewed with a pour-over (V60) or an AeroPress. Origin: Yirgacheffe, Ethiopia, washed process.

## Colombia ground coffee 250 g (34.99 PLN)
Pre-ground for drip coffee makers and pour-over, medium grind. Balanced, with caramel sweetness. Ground coffee loses aroma faster, so we recommend using it within 3 weeks of opening.

## Storing coffee
Keep coffee in its bag with the valve closed, away from light, heat and moisture. Don't keep it in the fridge: it picks up smells and moisture. Whole beans taste best within 6 weeks of the roast date printed on the bag.

## Sencha green tea 100 g (29.99 PLN)
A Japanese loose leaf green tea. Brew 2 g (one teaspoon) in 200 ml of water at **70–80 °C for 1–2 minutes**. Boiling water makes it bitter. The leaves can be brewed two or three times.

## Assam black tea 100 g (24.99 PLN)
A strong, malty black tea from India, good with milk. Brew 2–3 g in 250 ml of water at **95–100 °C for 3–5 minutes**.

## Caffeine
All our coffees are regular (not decaf). We don't sell decaffeinated coffee at the moment.
