# Shipping and delivery

## Where we ship
We ship to all addresses in Poland. Orders to other EU countries are possible for coffee and tea only, because grinders and drippers are fragile and we don't insure them abroad.

## Delivery options and prices
- **InPost parcel locker:** 12.99 PLN, usually delivered in 1–2 working days.
- **Courier (DPD):** 16.99 PLN, usually delivered in 1–2 working days.
- **Pickup in our Poznań roastery:** free, ready the next working day after you get the pickup email.
- **EU shipping (coffee and tea only):** 39.00 PLN, usually 3–6 working days.

Orders over **150 PLN** ship free with a parcel locker or courier in Poland.

## When we send your order
Orders placed by 1 pm on a working day leave our warehouse the same day. Orders placed later, at weekends or on public holidays leave on the next working day. We roast coffee twice a week (Monday and Thursday), so whole bean coffee is never older than four days when it leaves us.

## Tracking
You get a tracking link by email as soon as the parcel is handed to the carrier. If the link doesn't work after 24 hours, write to us and we'll check it with the carrier.

## Damaged parcels
If a parcel arrives damaged, take a photo of it before opening and report it to the courier, or in the InPost app for lockers. Then write to us within 7 days and we'll send a replacement or refund the damaged products.
